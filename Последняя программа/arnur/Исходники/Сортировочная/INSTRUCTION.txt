0. QUICK START — YOUR FIRST HUMPING IN 2 MINUTES
-------------------------------------------------
  At the top right there is a yellow block «WHAT TO DO NOW». It always says what has to be done,
  and it has one big button that does it. The first time, simply keep pressing it:

    1) «▶ Push up from track П1» — the hump locomotive pushes the train towards the hump;
    2) «▶ Open: normal speed» — the hump signal lights yellow and green,
       the wagons go over the crest;
    3) then everything goes by itself: cuts (one wagon or a group of wagons) roll down the hump, the retarders
       brake them (tick «Brake automatically (ARS)»), the switches are set by GAC from the cut list.
       Watch the diagram: every cut comes to its own track 1–8;
    4) if the block turns RED and flashes, something is urgent (for example, a wagon ahead that must not be
       humped). Read the text and press the button in the block;
    5) humping is over — the signal turns red by itself. Wait for the next train (time can be sped up with «×4»).

  Once you are used to it, clear the ARS tick and brake yourself (section 8), then switch step 4 to «Manual»
  and set the routes yourself (section 9).


1. STARTING THE PROGRAM
-----------------------
  Run: готовая_программа\Сортировочная\Sortirovka.exe (no need to install .NET).
  The window opens full screen. Next to the program:
    ИНСТРУКЦИЯ.txt, НҰСҚАУЛЫҚ.txt, INSTRUCTION.txt — this guide in Russian, Kazakh and English
      (opened with the «Guide» button or the F1 key);
    the Knowledge folder — the knowledge base of the AI assistant (created by itself at the first question).
  The interface language is remembered (folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sortirovka).
  Every start is a new shift: time 08:00, 100 points, a train ready for humping is already on track П1.


2. WHAT A CLASSIFICATION YARD AND A HUMP ARE (IN BRIEF)
-------------------------------------------------------
  A classification yard breaks up trains and makes up new ones: the wagons for each destination are collected
  on their own track. A train goes through the yard like this:
    1) it arrives in the RECEPTION YARD; the train locomotive is uncoupled and goes to the depot;
    2) the inspectors (ПТО) inspect the wagons and release the brakes; a CUT LIST is made — which cut goes
       to which track;
    3) the HUMP LOCOMOTIVE comes to the rear of the train and pushes it onto the hump (PUSH-UP);
    4) on the incline before the top (the CREST) the uncoupler uncouples the wagons according to the list;
    5) a CUT (one wagon or a group of wagons for one track), having passed the crest, rolls down by itself;
       the hump switches send it to the right track of the CLASSIFICATION YARD, and the RETARDERS control its speed;
    6) wagons for one destination accumulate on the track; when a train is complete it is moved to the
       DEPARTURE YARD, a train locomotive is coupled, the brakes are tested and the train departs.
  The main things for the hump yard operator:
    — a cut must come to ITS OWN track;
    — it must approach the wagons standing on the track NOT FASTER THAN 5 km/h (otherwise there is an impact —
      damage to wagons and freight), but it must not stop far from them either («short roll», a «gap» is left);
    — there must be an INTERVAL between cuts so that the switches have time to move.


3. HOW THE TRAINING YARD IS BUILT
---------------------------------
  The yard is one-directional, the yards follow each other from left to right:

    line → RECEPTION YARD П1–П4 → push-up track → signal Г → CREST → 1ТП → switch 1 → 2ТП-А / 2ТП-Б →
    switches 2, 3 → switches 4–7 → CLASSIFICATION YARD, tracks 1–8 → DEPARTURE YARD О1–О4 → line

  Below the reception yard there is a bypass track, the depot (ТЧ) and the stand of the hump locomotive ТЭМ18.
  The trains are training ones, short: 6–10 wagons (so the wagons on the diagram are large and easy to see).

  Classification tracks (destinations) and train norms (how many wagons to collect before moving a train out):
      1 — North               norm 8 wagons
      2 — East                8
      3 — South               8
      4 — West                8
      5 — Factory             8
      6 — Port                8
      7 — Pick-up (local)     5
      8 — Repair (VChD)       5

  Hump profile (as in the design norms for a low-capacity hump, simplified):
      incline to the crest 10‰ — the wagons are uncoupled on it;
      high-speed section 45‰ (the steepest descent);
      1st braking position 1ТП — on 14‰, for all tracks;
      switch 1 — divides the tracks into two fans: 1–4 and 5–8;
      2nd braking position: 2ТП-А (tracks 1–4) and 2ТП-Б (tracks 5–8) — on 9‰;
      switch zone 1.5‰: switches 2 (tracks 1–4), 3 (5–8), 4 (1–2), 5 (3–4), 6 (5–6), 7 (7–8);
      classification tracks — 0.6‰, with a counter-slope of 2.5‰ at the end.
  Hump height ≈ 2.6 m, classification track length 314 m, reception yard tracks — 300 m.

---------------------
  TOP STRIP: name, clock, «Variant…» (section 14), time (⏸ ×1 ×2 ×4), «New shift»,
  «Guide», language, the «AI» button.

  DIAGRAM (top left) — a view from above, as on the panel:
    • hump track circuits: grey — free, RED — occupied;
    • at a switch the direction it lies in is drawn brightly; a flashing yellow circle with a number — the switch
      is moving;
    • braking positions — thick bars: grey — released, orange — braking;
    • signal Г at the crest, its repeater ПГ above the reception yard, shunting signals М1–М4 at the end of the
      reception yard tracks (lunar white — push-up from this track is set);
    • wagons — coloured bars by type (pale — empty), they run on the rails and bend together with the track
      on curves; a red line on a wagon — mark «Л» or «Е»; locomotives — with a coloured stripe;
    • track labels: «П1 · №2001 · 8 wagons · ready for humping», «1 North · 5/8» (collected / norm;
      green — the norm is reached).
  SIDE VIEW (under the diagram) — the hump profile (height exaggerated): you see the train approach the crest,
    the cuts pass it and roll; above every cut — «number → track · speed km/h»
    (a red label — the cut has no route or is going to the wrong track).
  LOG (bottom left) — everything that happens; red lines are violations and refusals («Not possible: …»).


5. THE RIGHT PANEL: WHAT EACH PART DOES
---------------------------------------
  The panel goes from top to bottom in the order in which you work. The group needed now is highlighted
  with a yellow title.

  «WHAT TO DO NOW» (yellow block) — a hint and the main button. A red flashing block — urgent.
      Below it there may be a red line «Attention: …» — an early warning (for example, an «Л» comes in one cut).
  «Step 1. Push the train onto the hump» — buttons П1–П4. A GREEN button — the train on this track is ready for
      humping, blue — push-up from this track is in progress. Under the buttons — the state of the push-up.
  «Step 2. Hump signal Г» — four buttons: «■ STOP» (red), «Slow» (yellow, 3 km/h),
      «Normal» (yellow and green, 4.5 km/h), «Fast» (green, 6 km/h). A white frame — what is lit now.
  «Step 3. Braking the cuts» — the tick «Brake automatically (ARS)». Without it — for every braking position
      (1ТП, 2ТП-А, 2ТП-Б) there are three buttons: «Release», «Light», «Strong» and a display: which cut, its speed
      and «needed at most …» — the speed that is needed.
  «Step 4. Which track the cut goes to» — «Automatic from the list (GAC)» or «Manual», buttons for tracks 1–8 with
      names, «Clear the last route» and a hint which cut is next.
  «Cut list» — the table of the cuts of the train (section 12).
  «Shunting» — visible only in the «Hump + shunting» variant (section 14).
  «▸ Extra commands» — press to open: «Lower with locomotive», «Pull back (R + «Н»)»,
      «Push cut through», «Compact track…» (section 15). They are rarely needed — the main button offers them
      by itself when they are.
  «Shift score» — grade, points, number of violations, how many wagons were humped and sent.
  If the panel does not fit in height — scroll it with the mouse wheel.
  Every button has a tooltip: hold the mouse over it.


6. STEP 1 — PUSHING THE TRAIN UP
--------------------------------
  1) Find the GREEN button П1…П4 (at the start of a shift — П1) and press it. The shunting signal of this track
     lights lunar white, this train appears in the list.
  2) Look at the list: which cuts are ahead, are there marks «Л», «Е».
  3) Go to step 2 — while the signal is red, the train stands.
  Push-up from another track can be set only when humping of the current train is over.


7. STEP 2 — HUMP SIGNAL Г AND HUMPING SPEED
-------------------------------------------
  Buttons and indications (Instruction on signalling in railway transport of the Republic of Kazakhstan, cl. 99):
    «Fast»     — GREEN — humping at the set speed                                      — 6 km/h;
    «Normal»   — YELLOW AND GREEN — at an intermediate speed                            — 4.5 km/h;
    «Slow»     — YELLOW — at a reduced speed                                            — 3 km/h;
    «■ STOP»   — RED — Stop! Humping is forbidden (Esc key);
    «Pull back» (in «Extra») — RED AND A WHITE «Н» — pull the wagons back from the hump into the reception yard.
  Until the first wagon reaches the crest, the train is drawn up faster (up to 9 km/h), then — at humping speed.
  The repeater ПГ above the reception yard shows the same as Г.
  How to choose the speed:
    • usually — «Normal»;
    • the faster the humping, the closer the cuts are to each other — the switches may not have time (catch-up,
      «wrong track»);
    • single wagons one after another to neighbouring tracks, a «good runner» behind a «poor» one — «Slow»;
    • «Fast» — only when the cuts are long and go to different fans (1–4 and 5–8).
  The hump locomotive does not go over the crest — it stops at the top by itself.


8. STEP 3 — BRAKING THE CUTS (RETARDERS AND ARS)
------------------------------------------------
  Braking positions: 1ТП (all tracks), 2ТП-А (tracks 1–4), 2ТП-Б (tracks 5–8).
  WITH THE TICK «Brake automatically (ARS)» (as at the start of a shift) — the retarders brake every cut by
  themselves, the buttons are grey. The display still shows what is happening.
  WITHOUT THE TICK — brake yourself with three buttons:
    «Release» — no braking;
    «Light»   — light braking;
    «Strong»  — strong braking.
  A pressed button stays on until you press another one.
  The display to the right of the buttons: «cut 5: 16.4 km/h, needed at most 12.0» — the cut on the retarder and
  the speed it must be braked to (then it reaches the wagons on its track and couples softly).
  HINT: the button that has to be pressed now flashes with a YELLOW FRAME, and the display says «press «Strong»».
  An orange display — the cut is faster than needed.
  Rules:
    • brake until the speed is «needed at most …», and then «Release» at once;
    • do not leave braking on — the next cut will stop on the retarder;
    • brake loaded wagons («good runners») harder; empty ones («poor runners») often need no braking at all;
    • 1ТП holds back fast cuts and makes an interval if a cut is catching up with the one ahead.
  ARS does not save you from humping too fast: the intervals are set by the push-up speed (step 2).


9. STEP 4 — ROUTES OF THE CUTS (GAC OR MANUAL)
----------------------------------------------
  «Automatic from the list (GAC)» (as at the start of a shift) — the automatic hump interlocking takes the route
    of every cut from the cut list by itself. To correct it: select the cut in the list (while it is still in the
    train) and press the button of the right track.
  «Manual» — the routes already taken from the list are erased, and before every cut, until it passes the crest,
    press the button of its track (on the panel, keys 1–8 or a click on the classification track on the diagram).
    Under the buttons it says: «Next without a route: cut 3 — by the list track 5. Press button «5»»,
    and the right button is highlighted blue with a white frame. The route goes to the next cut without a route.
    Made a mistake — «Clear the last route». Routes can be entered in advance for several cuts ahead.
    If a cut comes to the crest without a route, the «What to do now» block turns red.
  GAC moves the switches by the routes itself when a cut approaches a switch and its section is free.
  A cut that has left the crest without a route goes where the switches lie — a violation.


10. HUMP SWITCHES
-----------------
  Switches 1–7 are hump switches: they move in 0.6 s. «Plus» — towards the tracks with lower numbers,
  «minus» — towards higher ones.
  To move one by hand — click the switch number on the diagram (the tooltip shows where it lies).
  It cannot be moved while a wagon is on the switch section (the section is red) — the program refuses.
  If a switch has started to move and a cut runs onto it — «movement under a wagon», a serious violation.
  So do not touch the switches by hand in front of an approaching cut — let GAC work.


11. WAGONS THAT MUST NOT BE HUMPED («Л», «Е»)
---------------------------------------------
  In the list in the «Mark» column and with a red line: «Л» — a wagon with people (conductors, escorts),
  «Е» — a tank with liquefied gas. Such wagons are not humped — they are lowered with the locomotive.
  They stand at the rear of the train. The program prompts you:
    1) in advance, when such a wagon comes after the first cut — a red line «Attention: …»;
    2) when it is first at the crest, the «What to do now» block turns red: press «■ STOP (red)»;
    3) the train has stopped — press «Lower with locomotive» (in the block or in «Extra»);
       the signal opens «Slow» by itself: the locomotive lowers the wagons to their track at no more than 5 km/h,
       couples them and returns to the crest.
  Humping such a wagon off the hump is a violation (−15).


12. THE CUT LIST
----------------
  Columns: № — cut number; Cars — number of wagons; Type — wagon types (Кр×2 — two box cars); t — gross mass;
  Run — rolling properties: «good» (good runner, rolls far — usually loaded), «avg.», «poor»
  (poor runner — usually empty); Track — the track by the make-up plan; Route — the route set;
  Mark — special marks «Л», «Е»; State — in the train / first at the crest / rolling / on track N / coupled…
  A blue line — the cut first at the crest, yellow — the selected one (click on a line), red — forbidden for
  humping or gone to the wrong track. The list shows the train being humped now (or the next ready one).


13. WAGONS AND LOCOMOTIVES
--------------------------
  The type by the first digit of the wagon number:
    Кр — box car (2),                   Пв — gondola (6),           Пл — flat car (4),
    Кп — container flat car (4),        Цс — tank car (7),          Хп — grain hopper (9),
    Рф — refrigerator car (8),          Цг — gas tank car (7, mark «Е»),
    Сл — wagon with people (9, mark «Л»).
  A loaded wagon is heavier and rolls better; an empty one rolls worse. Every wagon has its own running resistance.
  Locomotives: ТЭ33А (freight diesel) and KZ8A (freight electric, two sections) — haul trains;
  ТЭМ18 — a shunting diesel, here the hump locomotive: it pushes trains onto the hump and moves trains out to the
  departure yard.


14. WORK VARIANTS AND TASKS
---------------------------
  The «Variant:» buttons under the top bar of the window (the pressed one is blue):
    «Hump only» — everything before and after the hump is done by itself: train reception, uncoupling of the train
      locomotive, ПТО inspection (2 min), the hump locomotive coming to the rear of the train, its return after
      humping, moving complete trains out to the departure yard, departure. Your job — push-up and humping.
    «Hump + shunting» — the «Shunting» group appears on the panel, and you also do these yourself:
      — «Hump locomotive to the train…» → choose track П1–П4 (the train there is «waiting for hump loco»);
      — «Return hump locomotive» — after humping, from the crest to its stand (otherwise it will not go to the
        next train);
      — «Move train out…» → choose a classification track (when the norm is reached the track label is green).
    «Training» — you do EVERYTHING, the program checks every step and gives hints in the «What to do now» block.
      ARS and GAC are off at the start (you may switch them on, but it is better to learn by hand). Your steps:
      — reception: a train approaches the home signal (a blinking label on the scheme) — in the group «Receiving,
        brake skates, departure» press «Receive on: П1…П4» (a free track of the receiving yard);
      — the ПТО inspection runs by itself (2 min), then — hump locomotive to the train, push-up, humping, routes,
        braking;
      — brake skates (skid shoes): on an EMPTY classification track the skate is laid before the first cut — the
        track button in the «Skates on tracks» row (or right click on the track → «Lay brake skate»). On the scheme
        the skate is an orange wedge at the end of the track. A cut running onto it at no more than 12 km/h is
        stopped; faster — the skate is knocked off;
      — the norm is reached — remove the skate and move the train out to the departure yard;
      — a train on track О… — «Order locomotive»; the locomotive came and the brakes are tested (the train is
        «ready to depart») — «▶ Dispatch».
    «Exam» — like «Training», but without hints: the «What to do now» block, button highlighting and the AI
      assistant are off. The grade depends on violations.
    «Demonstration» — the autopilot does everything, the log lines «Autopilot: …» say what it does and why.
      The panel buttons do not work; time can be sped up. Good to show before the first lesson.
  «Hump only» ⇄ «Hump + shunting» switch at once; «Training», «Exam», «Demonstration» start a new shift
  (the program asks). If trains are not moved out, the classification tracks fill up.

  TASKS (LESSONS). The «Tasks…» button — a list of lessons: what to do, in how many minutes, restrictions (for
  example, ARS is off and cannot be switched on). Type the surname and name, choose a task, «▶ Start task». At the
  top of the window — the task, the time left and the goals (✓ — done). The lesson ends by itself when the goals are
  met or the time is over; «Finish task» — earlier. The result (goals, violations, points, grade) is shown in a
  window and saved as a file to «Documents\Сортировочная — результаты» — it can be given to the teacher. Goals not
  met — «2».
  Tasks:
    1. First humping — «Hump only», ARS and GAC, one train, 20 min.
    2. Manual braking — ARS off, two trains, no more than 3 violations, 45 min.
    3. Manual routes — GAC off, two trains, no more than 2 violations, 30 min.
    4. Cars that must not be humped — «Л» or «Е» in every train, two trains, no more than 1 violation, 45 min.
    5. Hump and shunting — three trains and one train moved out, 45 min.
    6. Full cycle (training) — variant «Training»: hump two trains, dispatch one, 50 min.
    7. Exam: DSPG shift — variant «Exam»: hump three trains, dispatch two, 70 min.
  A task has the same set of trains for all students — results can be compared.


15. EXTRA COMMANDS: FIXING MISTAKES
-----------------------------------
  Open «▸ Extra commands» on the panel:
  «Lower with locomotive» — for wagons «Л» and «Е» (section 11).
  «Pull back (R + «Н»)» — the train back from the hump into the reception yard.
  «Push cut through» — a cut has stopped in the switch zone (in the list «stopped in the switch zone!»):
    first «■ STOP» (push-up stopped), then this button — the cut is pushed onto its track.
    While it stands there, the next cuts will crash into it. The «What to do now» block prompts both steps.
  «Compact track…» (or right-click a classification track → «Compact track») — after short rolls «gaps» stay on
    the track; a shunting locomotive pushes the wagons to the end of the track. Not allowed while a cut is rolling
    onto the track.
  A cut has stopped on a retarder — press «Release», it rolls on by itself.
  Right-click a classification track — a menu: route a cut to this track, compact the track, move a train out.


16. VIOLATIONS AND SCORE
------------------------
  A shift starts with 100 points. Violations (red lines in the log) take points away:
    impact while coupling faster than 5 km/h                −5  (faster than 8 km/h — −10)
    cut passes the clearance post (end of track > 5 km/h)   −5
    cut came to the wrong track                             −5
    catch-up of one cut by another in the switch zone       −5
    cut stopped in the switch zone                          −5
    humping a wagon marked «Л» / «Е»                        −15
    switch moved under a wagon                              −10
    cut went without a route                                −3
    cut stopped on a braking position                       −3
    short roll: a gap of more than 50 m to the wagons
               (on an empty track — more than 100 m to the end of the track) −2
  Only in the «Training» and «Exam» variants:
    a cut went to an empty track without a brake skate              −2
    the skate was knocked off (run onto at more than 12 km/h)       −5
    a train waited at the home signal > 3 min with a free receiving track  −3
    a train waited > 3 min — no train locomotive ordered            −2
    a train was ready > 3 min but not dispatched                    −3
    a track overfilled (norm + 4 wagons) — the train not moved out  −3
    a train moved out short (less than the norm)                    −2
  Grade: 90 points and more — «5», 75 — «4», 60 — «3», less — «2».
  «Not possible: …» in the log is a refused command (the program explains why), no points are taken for it.
  A new shift — the «New shift» button (score and log are reset, the wagons in the trains are different).


17. TIME, LANGUAGE, AI ASSISTANT
--------------------------------
  Time: ⏸ — pause (or Space), ×1 — real time, ×2, ×4 — faster. Humping one train in real time takes
  2–4 minutes. Reception, inspection (2 min) and departure are sped up.
  Language: the list at the top (Русский, Қазақша, English) — changes at once.
  AI assistant — the «AI» button (ЖИ in Kazakh, ИИ in Russian). It answers in Russian or Kazakh — in the language
  of the question. Example questions (in Russian):
    «как распустить состав?», «что значит жёлтый и зелёный?», «как тормозить замедлителем?», «что такое АРС?»,
    «маршрут отцепу на путь 5», «что за стрелка 3?», «для чего 2ТП?», «почему недокат?».
  Point the mouse at a switch, a retarder, a track or a cut and ask «что это?». After a refusal or a violation
  the assistant suggests what to do by itself. Without the internet and a neural network it answers with ready
  texts from the Knowledge folder.


18. KEYS AND MOUSE
------------------
  Esc            — «■ STOP»: hump signal red (stop the push-up)
  1–8            — route of the cut to track 1–8 (like the track buttons in step 4)
  Space          — pause / continue
  F1             — this guide
  Click on a switch number             — move the switch
  Click on a classification track      — route the cut to this track
  Click on a cut on the diagram        — select it (yellow frame on the diagram and in the side view)
  Click on a line of the list          — select a cut in the train (then a track button changes its route)
  Right-click a classification track   — shunting menu
  Mouse hover                          — tooltip (switch, retarder, track, cut, signal, any button)


19. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
------------------------------
  I do not know what to press
     — read the yellow block «What to do now» and press its button. The group highlighted in yellow is the one
       needed now.
  The signal does not open («Not possible: set the push-up route first»)
     — first step 1: the green button П1…П4. The train on the track must be «ready for humping».
  The train is «waiting for hump loco» and stands
     — in the «Hump + shunting» variant — «Hump locomotive to the train…»; return the locomotive from the crest first.
  A cut went to the wrong track
     — in manual mode the route was not set or was set wrongly; or the cuts went too close and the switch did not
       have time (press «Slow» in step 2).
  Impacts while coupling
     — brake down to «needed at most …» (the button in the yellow frame) or tick ARS.
  Short rolls
     — do not brake empty wagons without need; close gaps with «Compact track».
  A cut stopped in the switch zone
     — «■ STOP», then «Push cut through» (the «What to do now» block offers both buttons).
  Trains are not moved out
     — in the «Hump only» variant a train is moved out by itself when the norm is reached, nothing is rolling onto
       the track and a departure yard track is free; in the «Hump + shunting» variant — «Move train out…».


20. FOR THE TEACHER
-------------------
  Suggested order of lessons — tasks 1–7 (the «Tasks…» button, section 14): from getting acquainted with ARS and
  GAC to the exam. Before the first lesson you can show the «Demonstration» variant. The student types the surname,
  the lesson result is saved to «Documents\Сортировочная — результаты» (file name — date, student, task).
  Your own tasks — in the source Исходники\Сортировочная\Sortirovka\Yard\YardTasks.cs (list All: variant,
  minutes, goals, ARS / GAC, «Л» / «Е», the number of the train set).
  Texts of the AI assistant — the Knowledge folder next to the program (Справка_по_интерфейсу.txt, Глоссарий.txt,
  Подсказки_по_ошибкам.txt): they can be extended, the program re-reads them by itself. Questions the assistant
  found no answer to are written to Knowledge\_Вопросы_без_ответа.txt.
  Humping speeds, train norms, track destinations, train length — in the source
